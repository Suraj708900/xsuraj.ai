How it works

  1. 1

    We set it up for your practice

    We configure the assistant with your hours, services, insurance information, and emergency instructions.

  2. 2

    It answers when you can't

    Missed and after-hours calls are forwarded to the AI, which greets callers, answers FAQs, and takes appointment requests.

  3. 3

    Your team follows up

    You receive each request with the caller's details so your team can confirm the appointment.

What it does

  • 24/7 call answering

    Picks up missed calls during busy hours and every call after hours, weekends, and holidays.

  • Appointment requests

    Collects the caller's name, contact details, and reason for visit for your team to confirm.

  • Answers common questions

    Handles FAQs like office hours, location, and accepted insurance using information you provide.

  • Emergency routing

    Directs callers with emergencies to 911 or your designated emergency line.

  • Transparent by design

    Tells every caller it is an AI assistant and that calls may be recorded.

  • Natural, friendly voice

    Speaks in a calm, professional tone that fits your practice.

Frequently asked questions

Can it handle patient data (HIPAA)?

A HIPAA-eligible setup for handling patient data is available on request, at extra cost. The standard setup and all demos use fictional data only.

Does it replace my staff?

No. It covers missed and after-hours calls so your front desk can focus on patients in the office.

Does it book appointments directly?

By default it takes appointment requests for your team to confirm. Direct calendar booking is available on request.

What happens with emergencies?

Callers describing an emergency are directed to 911 or your designated emergency line.

Do callers know they're talking to an AI?

Yes. The assistant tells callers it is an AI and that calls may be recorded.
