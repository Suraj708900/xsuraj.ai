Your next opportunity may start with a phone call

From dental practices to restaurants and service businesses, teams get busy and callers may not wait. Dental industry research below illustrates why reliable call handling can matter.

How it works

  1. 1

    Set up around your business

    We prepare approved business hours, services, FAQs and escalation rules. Dental clinics can request specialty-specific insurance and emergency instructions.

  2. 2

    It answers when you can't

    Missed and after-hours calls are forwarded to the AI, which greets callers, answers FAQs, and takes appointment requests.

  3. 3

    Your team follows up

    Your team receives captured requests and decides next steps; only your staff or a separately configured booking system confirms appointments.

What it does

  • 24/7 call answering

    Picks up missed calls during busy hours and every call after hours, weekends, and holidays.

  • Appointment requests

    Collects permitted inquiry details and preferred appointment times for a staff member to confirm.

  • Answers common questions

    Answers approved FAQs such as hours, location, services and, for dental practices, accepted insurance information.

  • Escalation & safe routing

    Follows your approved escalation instructions. Dental or healthcare emergency guidance is configured separately and never substitutes for clinical advice.

  • Transparent by design

    Tells every caller it is an AI assistant and that calls may be recorded.

  • Natural, friendly voice

    Uses an approachable, professional tone tailored to your type of business.

Frequently asked questions

Can it handle patient data (HIPAA)?

A HIPAA-eligible setup for handling patient data is available on request, at extra cost. The standard setup and all demos use fictional data only.

Does it replace my staff?

No. It helps capture missed and after-hours business inquiries so your staff can focus on customers who need personal attention.

Does it book appointments directly?

By default it takes appointment requests for your team to confirm. Direct calendar booking is available on request as a separately scoped integration.

What happens with emergencies?

Where appropriate, the assistant follows business-approved escalation instructions. For US dental or healthcare situations, urgent instructions are configured separately; AI never offers a medical diagnosis.

Do callers know they're talking to an AI?

Yes. The assistant tells callers it is an AI and that calls may be recorded.
